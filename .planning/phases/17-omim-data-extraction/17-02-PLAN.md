<objective>
Display OMIM MIM numbers as clickable links in the variant table and upgrade the OMIM external link from gene search to direct MIM entry.

Purpose: Users see OMIM data inline in the variant table and can click to open the exact OMIM entry page for a gene's MIM number, rather than performing a gene symbol search. This completes the user-facing side of OMIM data extraction.

Output: Variant table has a dedicated OMIM column positioned after Gene. MIM numbers are clickable links to omim.org/entry/{mim_number}. The old OMIM gene search link on gene_symbol column is removed. Requirements OMIM-04 and OMIM-05 are satisfied.
</objective>
